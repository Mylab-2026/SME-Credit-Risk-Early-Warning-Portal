Bands used (illustrative)

DSCR ≥1.50=5, ≥1.25=4, ≥1.10=3, ≥1.00=2, else 1 (below 1.00 raises an alarm). Debt/equity ≥4=1, ≥3=2, ≥2=3, ≥1=4, else 5. Net margin ≥10%=5, ≥6=4, ≥3=3, ≥0=2, else 1. Sales growth ≥20%=5, ≥10=4, ≥5=3, ≥0=2, else 1 (repeat clients only). Bank credits ≥95%=5, ≥80=4, ≥60=3, ≥40=2, else 1; capped at 3 above 130%. Cheque bounces 0=5, 1=3, 2=1, ≥3=0. Missed installments 0=5, 1=4, 2=3, 3=2, ≥4=1, ≥6=0. Partial payments 0=5, ≥1=4, ≥3=3, ≥5=2, ≥7=1, ≥10=0. Succession Yes=5, No=0. Collateral by coverage ≥150%=5, ≥125=4, ≥100=3, ≥50=2, else 1; partially secured capped at 4; unsecured=1. Each factor contributes (score ÷ 5) × weight. Grades: A ≥80, B ≥65, C ≥50, D ≥35, E below 35.

Decision Matrix

Grade × EWS Status → one recommended action. The reference every credit officer and approver uses, so the same combination always gets the same call.

A Distress Sector match or a DSCR alarm on the Scorecard, or a Red EWS status, should escalate a client above the matrix’s default action regardless of Grade — flag it to the reviewing officer.
